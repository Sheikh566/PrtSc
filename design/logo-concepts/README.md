# PrtSc app icon concepts

## 1. Keycap

A beveled glass keycap nods to the Print Screen key that gives PrtSc its name. The ice-blue key sits on an indigo tile, with a dark capture glyph stamped into its face. At 16×16, the four brackets and center square still read against the pale key.

## 2. Selection

A mint-green selection rectangle with four square handles brightens the area inside it, echoing how PrtSc dims the rest of the screen during a capture. At 16×16, the bright pane still stands out against the deep teal tile, while the handles read as brighter corners.

## 3. Aperture capsule

A floating violet glass capsule pairs a frosted lavender shutter with a vermilion record dot to represent screenshots and screencasts. At 16×16, the shutter simplifies to a pale ring and the red dot stays distinct.

## Pick

I’d choose **Keycap**. The physical key connects directly to the PrtSc name and hotkey workflow, and the dark capture glyph holds up best at 16×16.
